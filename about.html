01 / Overview

What is Labto AI? (Labto AI কী?)

Labto AI is not just another rigid rule-based chatbot. It is a contextual, self-learning AI assistant built specifically for websites, software products, and e-commerce stores.

✕

Traditional Chatbots & Search

Standard search bars and legacy chatbots rely on exact keyword matches. When visitors ask complex questions or search for nuanced products, traditional bots fail, forcing users to sift through long documentation or wait hours for email replies.

✓

The Labto AI Advantage

Labto AI indexes your website content, product catalog, and knowledge base using advanced vector embeddings (RAG). It understands real human intent, recommends products accurately, and provides friendly, zero-hallucination answers in real time.
