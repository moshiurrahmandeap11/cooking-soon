01 / Overview

What is Labto AI?

Labto AI is a contextual, self-learning AI assistant built specifically for modern websites, software products, and digital commerce.

Traditional Chatbots & Keyword Search

Legacy chatbots rely on exact keyword matches. When visitors ask nuanced questions, traditional search bars fail—forcing users to sift through long documentation or wait hours for email replies.

The Labto AI Solution

Labto AI indexes your website content, product catalog, and knowledge base using advanced vector embeddings (RAG). It understands real human intent, recommends products accurately, and provides zero-hallucination answers in real time.
